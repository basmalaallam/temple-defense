# Guardians of the Sacred Sun

A 2D Ancient Egyptian temple defense game developed for the DMET502 Computer Graphics course.

Protect the temple from three waves of enemies by placing guardians across four lanes, collecting coins, and managing your units.

## Features

- Four battle lanes.
- Three guardian types: Temple Warrior, Royal Archer, and Shield Guardian.
- Three enemy types: Sand Spirit, Cursed Scarab, and Jackal Brute.
- Three waves containing 3, 5, and 10 enemies.
- Melee combat, ranged attacks, jumping, and ducking.
- Collectible coins and guardian purchasing.
- Introductory cutscene, pause menu, and victory/defeat screens.

## Technologies

- C++17
- OpenGL
- GLFW
- GLAD
- GLM
- CMake
